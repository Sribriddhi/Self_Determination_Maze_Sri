What the paper actually says

Autonomy & competence: Cognitive Evaluation Theory describes conditions that support or undermine intrinsic motivation (paper pp. 70–71; PDF pp. 3–4).

Controlling conditions: The authors discuss expected task-contingent tangible rewards, threats, deadlines, and pressured evaluation as potential underminers of intrinsic motivation (PDF p. 3).

Relatedness: Feeling secure and connected can also support intrinsic motivation (PDF p. 4).

Important: This game is an educational illustration, not a controlled experiment or scientific proof. Its two levels vary in several ways and a single player's feelings cannot establish causality.

Level 1 · Exploration Garden

Before you explore!

This round is all about exploring at your own pace.

Move using your KEYBOARD arrow keys OR the ON-SCREEN arrow buttons ↑ ↓ ← →.
There is NO countdown and NO time pressure.
Take whichever route you like, and explore freely.

Collect all 4 stars to unlock the exit, then guide your bunny to the door.

After finishing, you'll rate enjoyment and perceived freedom from 0 to 5.

Level 2 · Performance Lab

Before you begin!

You're now being evaluated on how quickly you finish the maze.

Use ONLY the ON-SCREEN arrow buttons ↑ ↓ ← →.
Keyboard controls will not work.
You have a 60-second countdown!
The timer starts only after you click OK. You can still finish if it reaches zero.

Collect all 4 stars, then reach the exit to earn your trophy.
